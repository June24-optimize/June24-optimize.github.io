林深时见鹿

There was a time when everything felt dark and endless

— like walking alone through a forest with no path ahead.

But one day, light finally broke through the trees.

This drawing is about that moment

— when after countless rejections, doubts, and sleepless nights,

something finally clicked, and hope quietly returned.

The deer isn’t just a symbol of luck, but of gentleness and courage

— a reminder that even when the road feels lost,

the forest will always open up to light if you keep moving forward.

With a complicated state of mind, the painting emerged chaotic and without a defined theme

Life is nothing more than a process built on countless trials and errors.
How would I ever know what’s right or wrong if I never tried?
From now on, I’ll simply make sure not to repeat the same mistakes.
I’ll take this feeling and turn it into a love letter to myself —
because no matter how things end, every outcome is still a gift to me.
So here’s to wishing myself well. 💌

Pont Neuf by the Seine River

This sketch is a detailed and evocative representation of the Pont Neuf by the Seine River in Paris. Created during my high school years as a celebration of turning 18, this piece captures the timeless elegance and serene atmosphere of one of Paris’ most iconic landmarks. The intricate details of the bridge’s arches, the surrounding historic buildings, and the tranquil boats docked along the riverbank reflect the tranquil beauty of late autumn or winter. The bare trees and the softly shaded water add to the overall sense of calm and nostalgia, making this sketch a cherished memory of a significant milestone in my life.

At the age of 6, my mind was filled with endless curiosity about the world around me. I often pondered the purpose of my existence and the significance of life itself.
